## What is KÖR?

**KÖR – Community Unified Resource Registry** (Hungarian: Közösségi Önjegyző Rendszer, CURR) is a shared ledger for communities and organisations. Besides money, it records the flow of other resources – such as working hours or produce – using double-entry bookkeeping, together with the supporting documents.

## What does it do?

- **Recording transactions:** date, amount, currency, description, the accounts and the organisation involved.
- **Attaching documents:** uploading an invoice, receipt, contract or other document to a transaction.
- **Review and finalisation:** an editor reviews and finalises the entries; a finalised entry cannot be changed afterwards.
- **Clear accountability:** the system records who entered and who finalised each entry.

## Who is it for?

Access is restricted: user accounts are created by the operator's administrator. There is no public sign-up.

- **Data entry users** record transactions and attach documents.
- **Editors** review and finalise the recorded entries.
